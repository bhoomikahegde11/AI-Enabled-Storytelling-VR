# Tutorial and Level 1 market consistency audit

## A. Executive summary

Anubhava takes a player from exploring Hampi to running a spice stall in a timed market day. This audit assumes a fresh save and a player following the enabled build flow in sequence. Findings come from C#, serialized scenes and prefabs, dialogue assets and voice mappings. Source paths in the tables are relative to [Assets/_Project](../../unity/StorytellingVR/Assets/_Project), unless stated otherwise.

1. **P0 · The price lesson is on a different scale.** Pepper, cardamom and cinnamon are taught at 12, 18 and 20 varahas per Veesai. Live base costs are **112, 140 and 112**. The tutorial’s deliberately high 70-varaha cardamom example falls into the live ordinary seller-price branch’s suspiciously low range for one Veesai. The tutorial also presents 18 as a profitable outcome despite its own 18 cost.
2. **P0 · The X Spice Cost Guide has a confirmed visibility and scene-lifetime problem.** NewSpiceScene contains the wired prefab, but the demonstration body is commented out and the guide is unlocked about one second before leaving. NewTransactionTutorial contains an inactive copy. The main scene has no instance. The main **Y Current Trade** panel is a separate view.
3. **P0 · “Profit @ Offer” has a different basis from actual settlement.** The main panel subtracts market value. Settlement uses base cost. For one Veesai of cardamom and a 175-varaha offer, the panel can show **−35** while the settlement formula reports **+35**.
4. **P0 · Very low practice offers pay twice.** The under-cost branch credits the submitted amount in two places and awards more reputation than the fair branch. The practice wallet and reputation are also independent of the live save.
5. **P1 · The tutorial misses the voice-to-fulfillment handoff.** LT capture and A confirmation are partly taught, but LT re-record, accepting/countering a live offer, and settlement after one correct scoop are missing. The tutorial shows mock credit/payment before the physical lesson. Both Current Trade panels have buyer and quantity text fields unassigned.
6. **Retain the useful lessons.** The coin exhibit, free-roam conversations, notebook story, scoop and wrong-spice practice are valuable. The notebook remains **18 varahas** in the epilogue. The recommended changes preserve most voiced lines.
