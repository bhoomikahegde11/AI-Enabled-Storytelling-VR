Current Findings

- The Indian-accent Piper voices tested so far sound female-coded or female-leaning.
- We are still missing a strong Indian English male offline voice.
- The best non-Indian male fallback voices so far are en_US_kusal_medium and en_GB_northern_english_male_medium.
- The best current Indian-accent options are the en_IN voice and the hindi_accent voice, but both should be treated as female-leaning until the team reviews them.

Pronunciation Review Notes

- Hampi: needs human listening check on the Indian-accent models.
- varahas: needs human listening check on all voices because it is a domain-specific historical term.
- clove: expected to be straightforward, but still verify clarity.
- seer: needs listening review because it is a short trade term that may be mis-stressed.
